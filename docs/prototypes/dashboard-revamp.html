Proof

The honest before and after. For each tool, ctx compares what happened when it left the output alone against when it actually trimmed it, judged by whether you had to correct the agent or re-read something soon after. Trimming a tool is safe only when the trimmed rate is not higher than leaving it alone.

Re-reads and corrections come from your real sessions, not a benchmark or an AI judge. Intervals are 95% confidence. We show nothing for the trimmed side until ctx has actually trimmed a tool enough times to be honest about it.

Activity

Everything ctx saw, and exactly what it did before the agent saw it. Nothing is hidden, and the full original is always kept in your transcript. This is the receipt behind every decision.

Each row is one tool result. "Left alone" means ctx only watched and recorded. "Trimmed" means it cut noise before the agent saw it, and the full output is still saved for you.
